Reels Factory — Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 9 November 2026

You can remove Reels Factory's access and delete stored data at any time:

  1. Revoke Instagram access: Instagram app → Settings → Apps and websites → Reels Factory → Remove.
  2. Revoke YouTube access: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Reels Factory → Remove access.
  3. Delete stored data: Email navanidharama@gmail.com with subject "Delete my Reels Factory data" from the email linked to your account. We will delete stored tokens, queued videos, and logs for your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Revoking access stops all future publishing immediately; already-published posts remain on your accounts under your control and can be deleted there.
