OneTake: the whole reel is ONE continuous camera move. There are no cuts: the camera travels from each scene to the next.
- Carry: every scene connects to the next one. The last thing said in a scene leads into the first thing of the next
  ("…and that image? You run it as a container."). Write the "say" lines so each one hands over to the next.
- One concept carries the film (one thing that transforms, one journey through scale, one chain reaction), not a catalogue of facts.
- Rhythm: vary scene length a lot. Mix 1-line "statement" punches (a short say, about 1 sentence) with longer explain scenes.
- Small elements, big ground: 2–3 items per list/flow/objects scene, very short on-screen words.
