Collage B-roll: editorial paper collage. Strong flat colour, black-and-white halftone cut-outs, cream paper cards.
- Pick the 2–4 most important ideas and give each a "broll" scene: ONE sharp visual metaphor for that idea
  (e.g. "a robot librarian pulls one card from a huge filing cabinet" for RAG). Use 3–6 physical objects, with no text, logos or UI.
  The spoken line carries the meaning; the picture is a metaphor for it.
- Every other scene: very short on-screen words ("statement", "compare", "flow" with 2–3 steps).
- Colour field per idea: orange (time, effort, urgency), mustard (tools, warnings), green (thinking, reset),
  purple (rules, memory), teal (judgement, teamwork, automation).
