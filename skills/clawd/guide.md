Claude Animation Base: Clawd (a small terracotta block mascot with stubby legs, arm nubs and two tall slit eyes)
acts out the idea in hand-painted cartoon scenes.
- Use 2–3 "clawd" scenes: the hook (Clawd meets the problem), one story beat in the middle (Clawd tries, fails or gets it)
  and optionally the bye. Every clawd scene needs an EVENT: something changes from start to end (wants, tries, fails, reacts, gets it).
- Show, don't write: a clawd scene has no words on screen. The emotion and action carry it; my voice explains.
- Cause, then reaction: give the reaction time. One clear action per scene.
- The other scenes are normal explain scenes (warm paper look).
