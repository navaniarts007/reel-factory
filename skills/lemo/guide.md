Lemo Opuscar: a film style from the Lemo library. Keep the style's grammar (look, type, motion) and build MY topic in it.
### lemo-blueprint
Engineering blueprint: everything is a technical drawing. Prefer "flow", "compare" and "objects" (parts and how they connect),
labels like a drawing's annotations, numbers as dimensions (only real ones).
### lemo-swiss
Swiss motion graphics: a strict grid, one typeface, one red signal colour. Very few words and big scale contrast:
"statement" scenes with 1–3 words per line, lists of at most 3 items, lots of empty space.
### lemo-keynote
Dark tech keynote (a product reveal): one line per screen, a lone giant number, a calm, confident pace.
Prefer "statement" and "chart"; reveal one thing at a time.
### lemo-crt
ASCII / CRT terminal: the reel is a terminal session. Prefer "terminal" scenes (real commands only), short "statement" lines
that read like log output, and flows like a boot sequence.
### lemo-riso
Risograph print: playful zine energy in two inks. Friendly words, "story" and "objects" scenes, punchy statements.
### lemo-dossier
Halftone dossier: a case file. Frame the topic as an investigation ("Case: why your app crashes"), "story" beats as evidence,
"compare" as suspect vs suspect, and the takeaway as the verdict.
### lemo-pictogram
Pictogram motion: a fast catalogue on the beat. Items come one after another, numbered (lists with the "number" marker,
"objects" scenes), with big bold titles and very short lines.
### lemo-whiteboard
Whiteboard explainer in blue and orange marker: classic explain scenes, hand-lettered.
