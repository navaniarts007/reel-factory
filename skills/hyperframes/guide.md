HyperFrames look: an editorial motion-graphics explainer, design-led, with type as the hero.
- Every scene is ONE idea with a clear typographic hierarchy: one big line, one small supporting line. Short on-screen words.
- Prefer "statement" (kinetic type), "chart" (a hero number) and "flow"; at most two "list"/"checklist" scenes in the whole reel.
- Vary rhythm: put a 2-second punch statement next to a longer explain scene. Never use the same scene type three times in a row.
- A number that matters becomes the hero (statement or chart), but only real, certain numbers (list them in facts_to_check).
