Reels Factory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Reels Factory is a personal content tool that creates short tech videos and, only after the account owner's approval, publishes them to the owner's own Instagram and YouTube accounts using Meta and Google APIs.

Data we access

  • Your Instagram account ID, username, and an access token you authorize (to publish Reels you approved).
  • Your YouTube channel ID and an upload token you authorize (to upload Shorts you approved).
  • The video files, captions, and titles you approve for publishing.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish your approved videos to your own accounts, to show you publishing status, and to keep logs of what was posted. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties, and we do not use it for profiling.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as secrets in the app owner's private environment/secret store. Videos awaiting publication are kept in private cloud storage configured by the owner. Tokens can be revoked by you at any time (Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately stops all access.

Your choices

You approve every video before it is published. Nothing is posted without that approval. To request deletion of stored data, see our Data Deletion page or contact us.
